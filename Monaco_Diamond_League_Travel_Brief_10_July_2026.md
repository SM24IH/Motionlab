**Event:** Meeting Herculis EBS (Wanda Diamond League)  
**Date:** Friday, 10 July 2026  
**Venue:** Stade Louis II, Fontvieille, Monaco

---

## 1. Executive Summary

This brief outlines a same-day itinerary from Nice to Monaco for the Meeting Herculis EBS athletics meeting. The plan prioritises a relaxed pace during the day, reliable public transport, and timely stadium entry via **PORTE G**.

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Recommended departure from Nice | After **10:30** |
| First event | **18:15** |
| Final event | **22:00** |
| Stadium entry | **PORTE G** (Stand: Populaires G) |
| Seating | Placement libre (unreserved) |
| Target return window | **22:45–23:15** (subject to live timetable) |

**Recommended approach:** Depart Nice from **10:30** onwards; explore Monaco via a fixed walking route (Casino → Harbour → Old Town → Fontvieille); dine near the stadium before the meeting; enter via **PORTE G** by **18:05**; allow a short interval after the final event before returning to Nice by TER.

> **Important:** Train times may change due to engineering works or event-related crowd control. Reconfirm all services on [SNCF Connect](https://www.sncf-connect.com) **24–48 hours** before travel and again on the day.

## 6. Stadium Access — PORTE G

### 6.1 Entry Checklist

- [ ] Use **PORTE G** only (as indicated on ticket)
- [ ] Populaires G is unreserved seating — select seats upon entry
- [ ] Arrive **17:50–18:05** for the 18:15 first event
- [ ] Ensure tickets / QR codes are downloaded and device is charged
- [ ] Carry water; confirm current stadium policy on bottles

### 6.2 Seating Strategy

Unreserved seating means earlier arrival improves seat selection. Higher tiers often provide a clearer full-track view; lower tiers offer closer proximity to the track. For groups requiring adjacent seats, arrive well before the first event.

Follow on-site signage to PORTE G upon reaching the stadium.
