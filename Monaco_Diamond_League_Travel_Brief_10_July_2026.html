  • Use PORTE G only (as indicated on ticket)
  • Populaires G is unreserved — select seats upon entry
  • Arrive 17:50–18:05 for the 18:15 first event
  • Ensure tickets / QR codes are downloaded; device charged

Seating: Higher tiers offer clearer full-track views; lower tiers are closer to the track. For adjacent group seating, arrive well before the first event.
